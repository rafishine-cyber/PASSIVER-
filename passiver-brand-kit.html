05 · Flat Component Rules

Buttons, Badges & Surface Standards

All interactive elements use flat tonal contrast instead of drop shadows, and every CTA button carries the signature arrow-up-right icon.

Button System
Explore verified experts Inspect fee disclosure
  • Full pill radius (`9999px`) with bold `21px` arrow-up-right icon.
  • No elevation shadows; hover shifts surface color + icon `(+2px, -2px)`.
Dos & Don'ts
  • Do: Maintain `1280px` max container width with `32px` desktop / `20px` mobile gutters.
  • Do: Use unfilled `26px` outline icons directly beside section `H2` titles.
  • Do: Keep header & footer backgrounds unified with `--bg-base`.
  • Don't: Add `box-shadow`, `text-shadow`, or divider borders on the sticky bar.
  • Don't: Introduce green or blue status pills; use `--accent-soft` + `--gold-ink`.
